Extract background context for the opening and middle turns of a conversation.
The seed question will be asked later. Identify privately what specific fact, list,
comparison, or decision the seed asks for, then leave that target out of both
the topic.

- Write a broad topic that supports several distinct questions about the setting.
  Do not turn the seed question into a noun phrase or paraphrase.
- Preserve both the setting and the broad subject category. For example, keep
  the city and "travel modes" when the seed asks about a specific connection
  between a transit system and other modes. Omit the exact relationship,
  filters, or requested result that distinguish the seed from other questions
  in that category.
- Before returning, check whether someone seeing only the topic could readily
  reconstruct the specific seed question. If so, broaden the topic.
- Use the seed question's language. Return only a JSON object with a nonempty
  "topic" string as its only field.

Good output: {"topic":"开罗的出行方式"}
Avoid a topic such as “开罗BRT的连接交通方式”, which reveals what the seed asks to list.
