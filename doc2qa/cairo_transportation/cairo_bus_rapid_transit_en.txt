5. BUS RAPID TRANSIT (BRT)
Source: https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/governorate-services/transportation-services/bus-rapid-transit/
Page last updated: June 2026. The portal describes electric buses on dedicated Ring Road lanes, approximately 10-minute intervals, station access by pedestrian tunnels or bridges, and displays showing arrival information. It describes a 106 km route with possible extension, 49 stations, 36 in phase 1, approximately 100 electric buses and estimated cost 9.5 billion EGP. It also mentions 24-hour operation in three shifts, park-and-ride, minibus access roads and connections to metro, trains and SuperJet; these are source descriptions/plans, not independently verified current service hours.

Al-Talbiya appears twice in the source table. The number of displayed entries and its stated 49-station project count do not match; the table should not be treated as a verified route sequence.
Fares stated: 4 stations 5 EGP; 9 stations 10 EGP; 14 stations 15 EGP. The first phase is described as Alexandria Agricultural Station to Police Academy Station. The source describes 76 km of first-phase Ring Road expansion, including Mariouteya/Mounib to Alexandria Agricultural Road and Mansouria/Fayoum intersection to Wahat Road; later BRT phases depend on further expansion. Four charging stations are planned, including one at Adly Mansour; the design includes main charging centers at the ends of the line.
