4. LIGHT ELECTRIC TRAIN (LRT)
Source: https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/governorate-services/transportation-services/light-electric-train/
Page last updated: 2026. The project links the New Administrative Capital with the Cairo/Giza area. The source lists:
Phases 1 and 2: Adly Mansour; Obour; Mostakbal; El Shorouk; New Heliopolis; Badr. From Badr the page describes a north branch to Industrial Zone and 10th of Ramadan (1), and a south branch to El Robbiki; Capital Gardens (Badr El Gedida / New Badr); Administrative Capital (1) — Capital Airport; Administrative Capital (2) — City of Arts and Culture.
Phases 3 and 4 listed in the station table: Administrative Capital (3) — Cathedral of the Nativity of Christ; (4) — Strategic Command Center / The Octagon; (5) — Sports City; (6) — Central Station; 10th of Ramadan (2), (3), (4).

Tickets sold at counters or vending machines: 3 stations 10 EGP; 7 stations 15 EGP; more than 7 stations 20 EGP. Monthly subscriptions: 3 stations 300 EGP; 5 stations 500 EGP; 7 stations 600 EGP.
The page says phases 1–2 opened on 3 July 2022, covered 68.81 km and 12 stations, and ran from Adly Mansour to City of Arts and Culture. Adly Mansour connects LRT, Metro Line 3, railway, SuperJet and BRT. Branded minibuses connect nearby communities; parking and Capital buses linking City of Arts and Culture to the Government District are described. The page claims a 15-minute interval and free station parking; check current availability.
Phase 3, described as under construction: 20.4 km from Cathedral of the Nativity to Central Capital, 4 stations (3 elevated and 1 surface): Cathedral of the Nativity, Strategic Command Center, International Sports City, Central Capital. A high-speed rail interchange is planned at Central Capital.
Phase 4, described as under construction: 16 km north from New Obour to City Center, with West 10th of Ramadan, 10th of Ramadan and City Center stations. The source quotes 7.2 km elevated and 9.3 km surface, which do not add exactly to 16 km. The station naming differs between its table and phase description.
Phase 5: proposed 7.8 km extension, one station, from New Administrative Capital station across Cairo–Ain Sokhna Road to the capital's industrial zone; the page reports an MoU with AVIC.
The page describes overhead electricity supply, energy storage, parking and environmental/operating benefits; its comparisons are project claims, not independently verified here.
