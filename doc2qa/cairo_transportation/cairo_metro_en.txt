LINE 4 — construction/planning as described by the source, not confirmed live status: 42 km and 39 planned stations (37 underground, 2 surface), expected capacity about 2 million passengers per day. The route starts west of the Ring Road near 6th of October City, runs past the Grand Egyptian Museum and Remaya Square along Al Haram Street to Giza (planned interchange with Line 2), then to El Malek El Saleh (Line 1), Magra El Oyoun, Salah Salem Street, El Nasr Road, Al Mokawloon Al Arab Club, Nasr City and El Rehab, ending at an integrated transport center north of the Ring Road/Cairo–Suez Road junction.
Phase 1: 19 km, 17 stations, Hadayek El Ashgar to Fustat; via the museum, Remaya Square, Al Haram and Giza; crosses the Nile to El Malek El Saleh. Phase 2: 23 km, 22 stations, Fustat to Nasr City and New Cairo, via Salah Salem, Mansheyet Nasser, El Doweiqa, El Arab District, Al Mokawloon Al Arab Club, Railway Club, Al-Azhar University, El Shaheed Axis, Mostafa El Nahhas Street, El Teba, Mahdi Arafa Street, El Methaq Street, Zahraa Nasr City, the Police Academy and Mostafa Kamel Axis near El Rehab, ending near Cairo–Suez Desert Road and the workshop. The source does not give a complete 39-station list.

Application: visit a subscription office, complete the relevant form with two 4 × 6 cm photos, supply ID or birth certificate and any applicable membership documentation, and obtain the relevant authority's stamp; general public applications do not require a stamp. Student forms need Student Affairs approval, the prescribed photo stamps, ID/birth certificate, proof of tuition payment or current/previous academic-year card, and a phone number when required. Student passes cover the residence-to-study route. Passes are personal and misuse can incur a penalty.
Student form: https://www.cairometro.gov.eg/ (follow the form link from the source page).
